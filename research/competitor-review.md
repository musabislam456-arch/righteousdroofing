## Design choices made for RIGHTEOUS-D

- A deep charcoal/green, warm-paper and construction-orange palette, bold condensed headlines and restrained technical drawing grid create an architectural/field-notes identity rather than a generic contractor template.
- The three supplied services have their own editorial rows, not repetitive service cards.
- The supplied 11 review excerpts are displayed verbatim and attributed as provided; the 5.0/14 overall listing figure is marked as supplied listing information.
- The exact map embed, directions link and click-to-call number are surfaced in multiple obvious places.
- There is no WhatsApp action because WhatsApp support for the supplied number was not independently confirmed.
- Review mentions of Tarkwa and Kintampo are shown as customer-referenced locations, not a complete service-area list.
- No competitor images or stock photos are passed off as RIGHTEOUS-D completed work. The before/after art frames are original vector illustrations with visible disclosure labels; replace them with genuine company photos before launch.
